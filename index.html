How to Take a Photo That Can Be Analyzed:
  • Hold the camera 15–30 cm (6–12 inches) away from the affected leaf or plant stem.

        🔬 Image Understanding

        Visual feature extractors analyze leaf texture, chlorosis patterns, venation structures, and necrotic borders without manual feature engineering.

        Safety & Accuracy

        Safety & Accuracy

        Transparent, grower-centric intelligence designed to support informed decisions in the field.

        HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

        AI supports, does not replace, experienced growers or extension professionals. Final diagnostic authority and intervention decisions remain with certified agronomists.

        SUPPORTIVE TOOL

        Designed for early visual screening and rapid access to agronomic references, promoting thoughtful observation rather than destructive over-reaction.

        WHEN TO CONSULT AN EXPERT

        In cases of rapid disease spread, severe foliar collapse, unfamiliar symptoms, or commercial yield risks, immediately consult local agricultural extension officers.

        NON-DESTRUCTIVE INITIAL ACTIONS

        Start with sanitation, canopy airflow, physical isolation, and cultural hygiene before considering chemical interventions.

        Agricultural Decision Support Disclaimer

        "PaudhCare AI provides AI-based educational support and should not be treated as a professional agricultural diagnosis. For serious crop disease or treatment decisions, consult a qualified agricultural expert."

        Product Overview

        About PaudhCare AI

        PaudhCare AI is an AI-powered plant health support platform that helps users identify possible plant diseases from leaf images and provides sustainable crop-care guidance.

        Designed as a modern decision-support tool, PaudhCare AI combines visual pattern analysis with responsible agronomic best practices to support SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) by preventing avoidable crop loss.

        Developed as part of the 1M1B AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship.
